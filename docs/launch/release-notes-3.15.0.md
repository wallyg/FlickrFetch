# v3.15: release notes

Title for the GitHub release: **v3.15 — A better collage maker, and a click that opens the photo**

---

FlickrFetch 3.15 rebuilds the On This Day collage maker, gives the screens that show your photos by date the same buttons, and makes a click on a photo open it.

**A collage maker with one door and three steps**
- One "Make a collage" button on On This Day. Inside, the maker asks where the photos come from: one photo from each year, one year only, or the photos you ticked, and how many (up to 8, 6 for Polaroid).
- With fewer photos than years, the years are spread evenly so the oldest and newest always show. Every collage goes oldest first, with photos from the same year side by side.
- Three steps in place of one long panel: Style (five looks with previews made from your own photos, and the shape and size), Layout (arrangement cards with a drawing of each) and Photos (replace, swap, shuffle, public photos only).
- New arrangements: Mosaic (tiles of different sizes) and By year (a heading for each year). Arrangements that would look the same as another in the shape you chose are grayed out. "Matched" is now "Tinted".
- Replace a photo with another from that day, drag one onto another to swap, and move and zoom a photo inside its slot. Point at a small photo to see it larger.
- Every picture ends with your Flickr address and "Made with FlickrFetch".

**Share with links**
- Share offers the picture with one link for each year of the day (a year with a single photo links to the photo itself), or the picture alone. Copy has Copy image and Copy links.

**The same three buttons on every level of On This Day**
- The day, each year and the photos you ticked each get Collage, Download and Open. Open offers In FlickrFetch, On Flickr and In Flickr's organizer.
- The insights fold into one line you can open, and the page remembers which you chose.

**A click opens the photo; Select photos ticks**
- On This Day and the Timeline's days now open a photo in the app when you click it, like the Photostream. A Select photos button turns ticking on, and Done turns it off.
- On the Photostream, Select photos is the main button again (it had slipped under More), with Download all beside it.

**Smaller things**
- Chosen tiles and buttons no longer keep a dark edge after you pick something else, on every screen.
- American spelling throughout, with a test that keeps it so.

**Honest limits**
- The app is not yet signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS may ask you to open it once from System Settings, Privacy & Security.
- macOS asks for your Mac password about the keychain the first time you open an updated copy. Choose Always Allow.
- The address in the picture's footer uses your Flickr screen name, which for some accounts is not the same as the profile's web address.
- Flickr has no page for one date across every year or for a hand-picked set of photos, so On Flickr works for a single year only.

**Installing**
Open the .dmg and drag FlickrFetch onto Applications, replacing the old copy. Your sign-in and settings carry over.
