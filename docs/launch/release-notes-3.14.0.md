# v3.14: release notes

Title for the GitHub release: **v3.14 — A dashboard for your library, and one top for every screen**

---

FlickrFetch 3.14 turns the front page of Health Check into a dashboard of your whole library, and gives the main screens one shared layout.

**A dashboard on the Health Check front page**
- Health first: Tags, Photos and Albums each show a ring and how many have an Issue, a Warning or no problems, with counts and shares.
- Your library in numbers: how many photos, videos, albums and tags you have, who can see them (public, friends and family, private), and what is filled in (titles, tags, descriptions, locations).
- When you take photos: photos and videos a year or a decade (by date taken or uploaded), then by month, day and time of day. Photos with a guessed date are left out of these and counted in a note with an (i) that explains why.
- Where you take photos: your top countries and US states, worked out on your Mac from outlines kept inside the app, with no lookup online, plus how exact the locations are.
- What is in it: your most-used tags and biggest albums, with a note when several top tags sit on nearly the same photos (NY, NYC, New York), and a few records: your busiest days and months, longest streak, biggest upload day.
- Every figure opens the photos behind it, with a way back to Health Check. Save as PDF (the PDF button in the toolbar) keeps a copy of the whole page, with every view in it.

**Health Check says it plainly**
- No more "Fixed" or "Dealt with": a tag problem that is gone simply drops off your plan, and everything without a problem is "No problems".
- The rings are red, amber and grey (no green), so they work for red-green colour-blind viewers. Issues use a darker red.
- Every row reads tags then photos, and small rows still show a bar. Each photo is counted once, under its worst.

**One look across the app**
- The Timeline, On This Day, the Map and the Photostream share one layout: title and count on the left, the main button and a More menu on the right, filters below.
- On This Day's card now floats in the bottom-right corner instead of sitting between the bars, and "Across the years" shows your top 5 years with a button for the rest.
- One spacing scale, three heading sizes and one chip look everywhere, and no more one-off italics.
- Confirm download says what will be saved in each file and what will not. The About box has a calmer look, and About FlickrFetch is in your avatar menu too.

**Smaller things**
- Cmd+F on the Map opens Filters and the album box.
- The notice about different photo counts has an alert icon and a dismiss button.
- Settings wording tidied.

**Honest limits**
- The app is not yet signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS may ask you to open it once from System Settings, Privacy & Security.
- macOS asks for your Mac password about the keychain the first time you open an updated copy. Choose Always Allow.
- Country and state counts use simplified outlines, so a photo within a block or two of a border or coast can be counted on the other side.
- US states only: other countries' regions are not broken down yet.

**Installing**
Open the .dmg and drag FlickrFetch onto Applications, replacing the old copy. Your sign-in and settings carry over.
