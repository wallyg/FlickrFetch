Health Check overview

Mock-up for #220. The Dashboard tab uses the real figures from your library list, except the rings and the issue list, which are still made up. The other tabs are as built, with made-up numbers.

What changes

  • No "Fixed". A problem that is gone drops off your plan. Nothing counts as fixed.
  • "Dealt with" is gone. Tags you marked fine count under "No problems".
  • "Fine" is now "No problems".
  • No green. The ring is red (Issue), amber (Warning) and grey (No problems), so it reads for red-green colour-blind viewers.
  • One row everywhere: name, bar, then tags, then photos.
  • The two "Biggest" cards are gone from the Tags and Photos screens. They repeated the top of "by type". Issues and Warnings now use the same list.
  • One year chart, red and amber stacked, for Issues and Warnings together.

New: a library dashboard

The first tab, with your real figures. My advice:

  • Health first. It is the tab's job. One band of three rings, Tags, Photos and Albums, each with its Issue and Warning counts and percentages. The full lists are on the next screens.
  • Four headed sections: Health, Your library, When you take photos, What's in it. Thin rule and small capitals, like the roadmap board.
  • Year, month, weekday and time sit together under "When you take photos". Guessed or placeholder dates are left out of them, and a note counts them.
  • Photos and videos are named together in Health and in the date charts. The label follows the "Include videos in the checks" setting, so it says Photos when videos are left out.
  • No issue lists on this page. Cameras and collections wait.
