What this is, and what it is not

Good at

  • Any size of day: 400 photos is still one collage and a few lines.
  • Full-size viewing and every Flickr feature, with nothing to open or install.
  • Privacy kept by Flickr itself, so there is nothing for FlickrFetch to warn about.
  • Works the same on a Mac, a PC, an iPad and a phone.

Not

  • The picture is what you see offline. The links need internet to open.
  • People who are not your Flickr contacts see only your public photos.
  • Some places take only the picture or only the text (AirDrop sends just the picture), so there is a Copy the links button too.
  • The day-page address pattern is checked on a real account before this is built.
If this turns out to feel thin once your family tries it, the richer file (a PDF or web page with the pictures inside) is a later extra, and the options for it are on #267.
