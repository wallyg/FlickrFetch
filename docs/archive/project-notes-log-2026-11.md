- Filters are live everywhere (no Apply button). Flickr-backed searches (Photostream, People) debounce 0.6s; on-screen filters (Albums, Groups) 0.25s. User approved 0.6s, said not to go quicker. Old photos stay dimmed during refresh; skeleton only when nothing is on screen.
