# UAT 3.10: Photo Map (built-in outline plus the optional street map)

The map reads the library list already on this computer (69,069 of 71,108 photos have a location). It makes no new Flickr calls.
Run `npm run dev`, then work down the list. Tick a box when it behaves; write what you saw otherwise.

- [ ] M1 **Tab order.** Photostream, Albums, Collections, Tags, Timeline, On This Day, Map, then the divider and Health Check.
- [ ] M2 **First look.** Click Map. You see the world, numbered circles and a few dark dots, and a line at the top: "69,069 of 71,108 photos have a location · N in view".
- [ ] M3 **Move and zoom.** Drag to move. Scroll, the + and − buttons, double-click and the + and − keys all zoom (zoom keeps the spot under the pointer still). Arrow keys move.
- [ ] M4 **Circles split as you zoom.** A circle's number is how many photos it holds; zoom in on one and it breaks into smaller circles and finally single dark dots.
- [ ] M5 **Click a circle or dot.** A side panel opens with its photos (newest first), "Zoom in here", and "Download these N". Click a photo: it opens on Flickr. "Show more" appears when there are more than 60.
- [ ] M6 **Download these N.** The usual download box opens with the right number of photos. (You can cancel it.)
- [ ] M7 **Filters, the basics.** The top bar has a Photos / Videos switch and a **Filters** button (the same ones as the Photostream). The old year boxes and "Public photos only" are gone. Clicking Filters opens a drawer with Privacy, Tags, Album, Date and Located rows. When any are on, the button shows a number and chips appear under the bar with a "Clear all" link.
- [ ] M8 **The outline.** With the street map off (the default), countries are drawn under the dots with their borders. Zoom to a city: coast and land, no streets.
- [ ] M9 **It remembers.** Go to another tab and back: same place, same zoom, same filters, side panel still open.
- [ ] M10 **No outline file.** If the line "The country outline is not installed" appears at the bottom left of the map, the outline file is missing (tell me).
- [ ] M11 **Pointer over dots.** Move the mouse over a dot or circle: the cursor becomes a pointer (the pointing finger). Over empty map it is an open hand, and while you drag it is a closed hand.
- [ ] M12 **Street map, first time.** Tick "Show street map" (top bar). A short note explains what it does: OpenStreetMap is asked for the map images of the area you are looking at, nothing else is sent, the map images (not your photos) are saved on this computer. Click "Not now": nothing changes and the box stays unticked. Tick it again and click "Turn on street map".
- [ ] M13 **Street map looks right.** Streets, parks, water and place names appear under the dots. Zoom from the world down to a single street: the picture stays sharp and the dots stay on the right spots (a photo you know should sit on the right building or street). "© OpenStreetMap contributors" shows at the bottom right; clicking it opens their page.
- [ ] M14 **Street map is smooth.** Drag and zoom a lot. Squares may fill in a moment after you move, with the outline showing underneath meanwhile, but nothing flickers or leaves seams between squares.
- [ ] M15 **It remembers, and it saves.** Untick it: back to the outline. Quit and reopen the app, open Map: the choice you made last is still set, and no note appears this time. Look at a place you visited before: it appears at once. (The saved map images are in the app's data folder, `map-tiles`.)
- [ ] M16 **Offline.** Turn Wi-Fi off, open a place you have not looked at: the note "The street map could not be loaded" appears and the outline shows. A place you saw earlier still shows its streets. Turn Wi-Fi on: squares fill in within about a minute.

- [ ] M17 **Street map in Settings.** Settings > General has a "Street map" section: the "Show street map" switch (it matches the box on the Map tab: change one, close Settings, and the other follows), "Size limit" (a number in MB), "In use" with its size, and "Clear". The (i) next to the title explains what is sent.
- [ ] M18 **The size limit.** Type 5 or 99999 or "abc": the box turns red and says "Enter 20 to 5,000", and when you leave the box (or press Enter) the last good limit comes back. Type 50 and press Enter or click away: accepted. Close and reopen Settings: 50 is still there. While you are still typing nothing is saved.
- [ ] M19 **In use and Clear.** After looking around the map with the street map on, "In use" shows a size and an image count (reopen Settings to refresh it). Click "Clear": it asks "Delete them" or "Cancel". Choose Delete them: it reads 0 MB. Back on the Map, map images download again.
- [ ] M20 **Limit is enforced.** Set the limit to 20 MB, look around a lot of places (zoom in on many different spots). "In use" stays at or under about 20 MB (it trims the oldest once it passes the limit).
- [ ] M21 **Off means off.** Turn the street map off in Settings. Open the Map: only the outline. Nothing is downloaded (the "In use" number stops growing).
- [ ] M22 **Restore defaults** (Settings, bottom) turns the street map back to "never asked": ticking it on the Map shows the one-time note again, and the limit returns to 300 MB.

- [ ] M23 **Photos / Videos.** Click Videos: only video locations remain, a "Videos only" chip appears, and the line at the top reads "N match · N in view". Click All: back to everything.
- [ ] M24 **Privacy.** In the drawer pick Public, then Friends, Family, Friends & Family, Private. Each shows only that level (same wording as the Photostream). A "Privacy: …" chip appears; its ✕ removes it. Public should give the same photos the old "Public photos only" box did. In your library the located photos are 54,665 public, 32 friends, 177 family, 14,195 friends & family and none private, so Private should show an empty map with the "No photo with a location matches" note.
- [ ] M25 **Tags.** Type a tag you use (try one with a space, like "new york", and one with capitals), press Enter: only photos with that tag stay, and a chip shows it. Type two tags separated by a comma: **any** shows photos with either, **all** only photos with both. Hover the "?" for the explanation. A tag nobody has gives an empty map with the note "No photo with a location matches these filters".
- [ ] M26 **Album.** Click the Album box and type part of an album name: matching albums are offered (best matches first, with their photo counts). Pick one: a chip "Album: …" appears and only its photos show. Pick a second album: photos in either show. Remove one with its ✕. With an album and a tag both set, only photos that match both show.
- [ ] M27 **Date.** Choose Taken or Posted, then a range: Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This year, Last year, Since a date…, Between two dates…. Date boxes appear for the last two and the map updates as you fill them. A date chip shows the range. Photos with no date are left out once a range is set. Posted uses the day you uploaded the photo.
- [ ] M28 **Located.** The row has Any, Street, Town or better, Vague only; hover the "?" for what each means. Street should keep almost everything (most of your photos are street level); Vague only should leave a small number (roughly 1,400 of 71,000), often sitting in the middle of a country or region.
- [ ] M29 **Filters work together.** Combine, for example, Videos + a year + an album. Only photos matching all of them show, and the "N match" number agrees with what you see. Clear all resets everything.
- [ ] M30 **Click and download follow the filters.** With filters on, click a circle: the side panel lists only matching photos. "Download these N" offers exactly that many. Changing a filter while a circle is selected closes the side panel.
- [ ] M31 **Drawer and tabs.** Open and close the drawer: the map resizes to fit. Switch to another tab and back: filters are still set (the drawer too). Quit and reopen the app: filters are cleared (only the street map choice is remembered).

- [ ] M32 **Health Check: Vague location.** Open Health Check > Photos. Under Locations there is a new check, "Vague location", set to Warning by default. Its (i) note lists what it flags (precision 10 or less on Flickr's 1 to 16 scale) and what it leaves alone. It may need a Rebuild first if the list is older than the 3.6 scan. It should list roughly 600 photos (the country-level and region-level ones), usually pinned in the middle of a country. "Show on a map" is not part of this check yet (#227). "This one is fine" offers three reasons (as precise as the place gets; no exact spot applies; I will pin it exactly later), and Undo brings the photo back.
- [ ] M33 **Same numbers as the Map.** The check's count should be close to the Map's Located > Vague only count (the Map counts only photos that can be drawn; the check counts every flagged photo, so the check may be slightly higher).

- [ ] M23 **The world repeats sideways.** Zoom all the way out: the world appears side by side with copies of itself on the left and right, with no empty strip. Drag left and right across the edge as far as you like: it keeps going, with Fiji and the Aleutians continuing on the other side. A circle or dot near the edge shows on both copies, and clicking either copy opens the same photos. Turn the street map on and do the same: the street pictures continue across the edge with no seam or gap.

- [ ] M24 **Scroll and zoom gestures.** A two-finger swipe on the trackpad moves the map in any direction (up, down, left, right). Pinching zooms in and out. Cmd + two-finger scroll zooms too. Dragging with the pointer pressed still works, and so do the + and − buttons and the arrow keys.
- [ ] M25 **No blank space above or below.** Scroll or drag as far north and south as you can: the view stops at the top and bottom of the world and you never see an empty band above the Arctic or below Antarctica. Zoom all the way out: the world fills the window top to bottom (with copies at the sides), not a small map in a big empty box.
- [ ] M26 **The map fills the window and follows it.** The map is as tall as the space under the filter bar (not a short strip). Click a circle so the photo tray opens: the map narrows but stays the same height. Close the tray: it widens again, still the same height. Make the app window bigger and smaller, wider and narrower, taller and shorter: the map follows each change. Open the tray with lots of photos (use Show more): the map does not get taller.

- [ ] M27 **The map keeps its place when it resizes.** Zoom in on a place and look at something specific (a coastline, a circle). Click a circle near the right edge so the photo tray opens: the picture does not slide, the circle you clicked stays in view (it is nudged only if the tray would have covered it), and the left part of the map has not moved. Close the tray: the map widens to the right and nothing else shifts, so you are back where you were. Make the window narrower and wider: the top left of the map stays put and the new room appears at the right. Make it shorter with a circle picked near the bottom: the circle stays in view.

- [ ] M34 **A tab switch does not move the map.** Zoom in on a place, click a circle near the bottom right so the photo tray opens, then go to Albums and back to Map: the map shows exactly the same area, the circle is still picked and the tray is still open.
- [ ] M35 **Pinch zoom keeps up.** Pinch in and out on the trackpad, and use Cmd and scroll: the map zooms smoothly by about as much as your fingers moved, not in tiny steps.
- [ ] M36 **The street map catches up with where you stopped.** With the street map on, swipe quickly across a country at a high zoom and stop: the squares for the place you stopped at fill in within a second or two, not after a long wait for the places you passed.
- [ ] M37 **Big cache, no freeze.** With a fair amount saved (check Settings > In use), quit and reopen the app, open the Map with the street map on: the map stays responsive from the first moment.
